# E-WasteMart

E-WasteMart is an e-commerce platform dedicated to the responsible management of electronic waste (e-waste). Our mission is to reduce environmental impact by offering services for e-waste collection, certified recycling, and the sale of refurbished electronics.

## Features

- **E-Waste Collection**: Schedule pickups or find drop-off locations for your electronic waste.
- **Certified Recycling**: We work with certified recyclers to ensure responsible and eco-friendly processing of e-waste.
- **Refurbished Electronics**: Buy and sell refurbished devices, giving them a second life.
- **Donations**: Donate working electronics to those in need, supporting various charitable causes.

## Technologies Used

- **React.js**: For building a dynamic and responsive user interface.
- **Next.js**: For server-side rendering and optimized performance.
- **Razorpay**: For secure and seamless payment processing.
- **MongoDB**: For managing our database with flexibility and scalability.
- **AI Chatbot**: To provide instant support and assistance to our users.

## How It Works

1. **Sign Up** on E-WasteMart to create an account.
2. **Browse Services** for collection, recycling, or purchasing refurbished electronics.
3. **Schedule or Drop Off** your e-waste at a convenient location.
4. **Shop or Donate** electronics to contribute to our mission of reducing e-waste.
